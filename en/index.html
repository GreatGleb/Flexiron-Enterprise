Where a metal stockholder loses money while the books live in Excel

  • Offcuts drop out of the books

    The rest of a sheet or tube after cutting is recorded nowhere. Nobody finds it when it is needed — and a new one gets cut.

  • Margin shows up too late

    Order cost is put together by hand. The real profit becomes known at the end of the month, not at the sale.

  • Stock disagrees with the shelf

    Reservations, partial shipments and write-offs live in different files and never add up.

  • Purchasing — one email at a time

    Supplier prices are requested one by one and compared in the inbox. The request history gets lost.

Flexiron is designed from the working Excel sheets of an operating metal stockholder: metal stock, offcuts, invoices.

One loop: from buying metal to the order being paid

  1. Suppliers

    one price request to many

  2. Stock

    batches with their own cost

  3. Cutting

    the offcut stays in stock

  4. Order

    goods, services, margin

  5. Shipping

    in full or in parts

  6. Payment

    paid and still due

Analytics sees the whole loop. Profit and loss, receivables, stock shortages, reasons customers say no — without manual exports, because all the data is already in one system.

Stock: every batch with its own cost

  • Receiving in batches

    Supplier, date and purchase price stay with every batch.

  • Reserved for an order

    What is reserved will not be sold twice.

  • FIFO cost

    An order takes the oldest batches first — at their real purchase price.

  • Shortages

    Items below minimum stock — and the purchasing status of each.

Cutting: the rest after cutting is not lost

  1. 1

    Pick a batch: sheet, tube, profile

  2. 2

    Enter the pieces — length and quantity

  3. 3

    The system adds the kerf for every cut and shows what will be written off and what remains

The offcut becomes its own stock item linked to the source batch — it shows up in search and can be sold.

Order: goods and services, margin on every line

  • Services in the same order

    Cutting, delivery, packing — each with its own cost.

  • Margin and discount per line

    The manager sees what they will earn before anything ships.

  • Shipping in parts

    “Shipped 16 of 40” — the rest waits in reserve.

When an item is added to an order, the system itself suggests matching offcuts from stock.

Order money is calculated, not typed in

  • Cost

    Built from the order lines and stock batches. Nobody edits it by hand.

  • Markup, discount, VAT

    Set for the whole order, VAT by the chosen regime. The rate basis goes onto the invoice.

  • The total can be entered by hand

    Agreed a round sum with the customer — the system spreads it across the lines itself.

  • Paid and still due

    Payments are tied to the order, the customer’s debt is visible at once.

Purchasing: one price request to several suppliers

  • One email — to everyone

    The system builds the email from a template and sends it to everyone in blind copy.

  • Request history

    Who, when, what was requested and whether the supplier replied.

  • Supplier base

    Specialisation, rating, delivery times.

Analytics: profit and problems visible without exports

  • Management dashboard

    Stock value, receivables, revenue, profit, shortages and risk notifications.

  • Profit and loss

    From sales to operating profit — by line item, per month.
